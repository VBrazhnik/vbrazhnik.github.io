Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application “VBtracker” (package name: com.vb.tracker.free), available on Google Play (“the App”), developed by Vladyslav Brazhnik (“the Developer”). It explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Summary

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It does not contain ads, analytics, or any third-party SDKs, and it does not have internet access.

Information the App Accesses

VBtracker is a to-do and routine tracker. The tasks, routines, notes, and colors you create are stored only in a local database on your device, so the App can display and manage them. This information is never read by anyone but you, and is never uploaded, logged, or sent anywhere.

App Settings

Your in-app preferences (such as theme) are saved locally on your device using Android’s SharedPreferences. If you choose to enable the optional app lock, the PIN you set is stored on your device only as a salted, one-way hash — never in plain text — and is never transmitted anywhere. All of this data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

Data Sharing

The Developer does not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties, because the App does not collect or transmit any data in the first place.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
